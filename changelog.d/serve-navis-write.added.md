- `refdes serve` now answers `POST /api/navis/archive` and `POST /api/navis/restore` with a `{"navi": <selector>}` body (full key, unique key prefix, or `refdes navis all` ordinal). Success is `200 {"kind": "ok", "verb", "key", "changed"}`; re-archiving reports `changed: false`. Refusals are JSON: 403 on a `--no-write` server, 404 for a selector naming nothing, 409 `ambiguous` (naming the candidates) or `conflict` (keyless, not archived, orphaned record, pending journal), 500 on a store failure, 400 on a malformed body. A successful write rebuilds the preview before the response lands, so the Navis panel is current on the next page load.
