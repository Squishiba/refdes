- The browser editor's Navis panel gained **Archive** and **Restore**
  actions. Keyed active rows archive from the top list; archived keyed
  rows restore from Show-all. A 200 refreshes the panel in place and
  preserves any unsaved editor draft, while a failure re-enables the
  clicked button and shows the server's error. On a `--no-write` server,
  the first refusal locks every action for the session and keeps the
  refusal visible. Orphaned archived rows and keyless rows stay disabled
  or button-free, matching the published preview panel (slice N5d-2).
