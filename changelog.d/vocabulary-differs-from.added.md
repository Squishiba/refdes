- **`differs_from:` — disambiguation written into the standard** (vocabulary-review P20). A type, a field or a link verb can carry a mapping of confusable term to the one sentence that tells them apart, next to its `doc:`, in the bundled standard and in a project's `refdes-schema.yaml` alike. The generated vocabulary page renders each pair as a **Differs from** fact line under the term's definition (a field's line appears in the type's fields table), linking the other term where it has an entry on the page. A wrong shape is a configuration error naming the block path, and a term that does not resolve in the resolved standard — a type, a link verb under its own or its inverse name, a set, or a field of the same type — is a load error naming both sides, the posture `include:`, `extends:`, link targets and `required_when:` already keep. `refdes schema` carries the block as an annotation where present; a project that declares none renders and exports byte-identically. `hardware@3` lands the review's `alternate`/`drop_in` and `bound`/`limit` pairs.
