- An item that continues another with `follows:` now renders a **Thread**
  section on its page: a "currently concludes" panel — the thread's folded
  `status`, `rationale`, `options` and `checks`, plus its `satisfies`,
  `selects` and `constrained_by` links — with every value attributed to the
  entry it came from, and the whole thread as a timeline with the page's own
  entry marked. A forked thread concludes nothing: its panel says so and
  names the open tips. Items in no thread render exactly as before.
