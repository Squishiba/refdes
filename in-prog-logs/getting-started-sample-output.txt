## Not fixed, and why

- §5's `site written to /path/to/my-board/_site` is a placeholder path, not a
  literal capture. Left alone deliberately — it is the page telling the reader
  where their own project lands, and no real absolute path belongs in it.
- The notice is on stdout for both `id` and `build` (on stderr only for
  `index`, per `changelog.d/load-writes-everywhere.fixed.md`). The §5 block
  therefore shows the notice above the `WARNING`, inside the stdout group; a
  terminal that buffers the streams differently may interleave it elsewhere,
  which the page's existing sentence about streams already covers.
- Nothing else in the page is wrong as far as the walkthrough reaches. See the
  §7 list above — every claim checked out.
