Difficulties
------------
The main difficulties were process, not content: the review loop never
converged on the 839-line doc, and the log's practice of citing the commit
that fixed each review round produced <pending> placeholders and follow-up
"record sha" commits, which became a recurring source of small
inaccuracies in the log itself. Both loops were cut: the doc was rescoped,
and this log no longer records per-round history.
