## Design decision

Warning condition (all three): item's type is append-only with `sealing: history`;
a legacy seal record exists for it whose recorded hash no longer matches the
live content; the item has NO capture event in `.refdes/history`. Skip items
whose legacy record has a key mismatch (that is already an ERROR) and keyless
items (the deleted-key report owns them).

Why a legacy seal record as the "was it edited" evidence: it is the only prior
record of the content that exists for the population B3 describes (a project
upgraded from build-sealed to history-backed), it is the exact comparison
`audit` already makes in "Append-only entries edited after sealing", and it
cannot fire on ordinary post-release edits the way a baseline diff would.
A never-sealed, never-captured entry has no prior content on record, so there
is nothing to detect an edit against — that case stays silent (the report's
separate type-level "never captured" suggestion is not implemented).

Emit point: build.py, new `warn_uncaptured_edits(project)` called from `build()`
immediately after `warn_edited_after_captured(project)` — the one place that
makes it visible on both `refdes check` and `refdes build` (the report asks for
"the first check/build"), through the existing project.warn diagnostic flow,
which cannot move the exit code.
