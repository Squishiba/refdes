## What the semantic hash covers (R9-F2 investigation)

- history.semantic_payload (src/refdes/history.py) hashes
  {history_format, type, fields, links, body} with the body whitespace-
  collapsed. item.body is the raw markdown body, fence lines included, so the
  ```calc info string (and its id=) is hashed. capture_index
  (history.py:728-731) compares the live semantic_digest against each capture
  event's stored object digest; a mismatch is the warning (build.py
  warn_edited_after_captured).
- The digest is history's own (independent of build.HASH_FORMAT; module
  docstring §2 and the "not item.content_hash" paragraph). The label is also
  in build._hash_payload's normalized body (content_hash, HASH_FORMAT 5),
  which drives baselines/seals/uncaptured_edits — NOT touched here.

## R9-F2 decision: fix, per the task's gate

- The strip is contained: one regex in history.py normalizing every calc
  fence's opening line to the bare ```calc before the whitespace collapse
  (same line-start anchor as calc.CALC_BLOCK_RE, so indented fences stay
  prose). Only semantic_payload consumes it; nothing outside history.py
  computes the digest.
- Unlabelled items are byte-identical: for a body with no labelled calc fence
  the regex rewrites nothing (a trailing space after ```calc is absorbed by
  the collapse, which follows). Pinned by
  test_the_label_strip_leaves_unlabelled_digests_byte_identical, which
  rebuilds the PRE-strip payload formula ({..., _collapse_ws(body)}) in the
  test and asserts semantic_digest equals it for both the fence item and a
  plain one. Probe: captured digest 56ac18d3744c97d5… is the same value the
  pre-change code minted for the unlabelled body.
- No hash-format bump (HISTORY_FORMAT stays 1), no sealing/baseline touch.
- Known cost (recorded in the changelog fragment): an item captured *with* a
  labelled fence under the old digest shows one warning-only "edited after
  captured" line after upgrading; recapturing settles it. Inherent to any
  change that makes label edits invisible.
