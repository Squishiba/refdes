Feasibility note for a `standard upgrade`-style automatic migration of the
v3-pinned shape (recorded, NOT built -- scope per the task):
revise.py's apply() is driven by a revise.Mapping built from
migration.yaml, and the chain in cli.cmd_standard_upgrade walks
`from_version -> --to` strictly upward, refusing when the project is already
at `--to` (that is the exact `configuration error` reproduced above). The
v3->v3 "migration" needed here is a non-empty mapping
(merge_types/fields/links) applied *within* one pinned version, which is
outside the chain's model: it would need (a) an idempotent re-apply of the
v3 migration onto an already-v3 project (the chain has no such step and
baselines/seals are relabelled per step), (b) the same
refuse-and-rollback transaction (body-merge conflicts are handled by
revise.check_body_merge_conflicts, so that part is reusable), and (c) a
decision on the manufactured `follows:` edges, whose freeze-and-capture on
the first writable load is the fragment's documented behaviour. Scope: one
`standard repair --for-retired-types`-style command reusing revise.apply()
with v3's migration.yaml and no pin bump. Not built.
