## 1. revise.py snapshot gating — REPRODUCED, FIXED

Confirmed by control-flow trace of apply(): after the `if dry_run: return`
early-exit, the snapshot is read by exactly two consumers —
`_simulate_key_ensure` (dry-run + prefix_rename, the per-line diff report)
and `_collect_refresh_rewrites()` (its loop over `snapshot.items()`; both
call sites live after the dry-run return, so non-dry only). For a non-prefix
dry run nothing ever read it. Now gated:
`if not dry_run or prefix_rename`, `{}` otherwise; position unchanged (it
must be taken before `_run_key_ensure` mutates the tree on the non-dry prefix
path). Position preserved matters — the snapshot is the baseline the
refresh-rollback diffs against, and key-ensure rewrites are excluded from it
by `rel not in ensure_rewrites`, so taking it any later would corrupt the
`_collect_refresh_rewrites` classification.

Tests: tests/test_readonly_explicit_writes.py (19) + the three revise test
files = 115 passed. No behaviour change -> no new test required by the task;
the gate is guarded by tests from both sides instead: inverting the
condition makes 5 prefix-dry-run report tests fail
(tests/test_revise_keys_cleanup.py::test_dry_run_simulates_the_pipeline_and_writes_nothing,
::test_dry_run_changed_files_match_the_real_run_including_refresh et al.),
and skipping it on a real run trips
test_revise_rolls_back_refresh_writes_landed_by_a_non_prefix_mapping.
