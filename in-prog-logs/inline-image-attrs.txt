# Inline image attribute suffixes (chunk 1)

## Task

`{width=...}` on an image was silently dropped as literal text whenever the
image was not alone in its paragraph — `See this: ![alt](x.png){width=50%}`,
`- ![alt](x.png){width=50%}`. `FIGURE_RE` only matched a whole paragraph that
was nothing but one `<img>` plus the suffix, so `_apply_figure_attrs` never
saw the other positions and the braces reached the page as text.

## What was done

- `src/refdes/build.py`
  - `INLINE_FIGURE_RE` (build.py:74): `<img ...>` followed by `{...}` anywhere.
  - `_image_suffix_attrs` (build.py:1593): the suffix parse the figure path
    already did, factored out so both paths parse identically.
  - `_apply_inline_image_attrs` (build.py:1606): runs after `FIGURE_RE.sub`
    inside `_apply_figure_attrs` (build.py:1720-1721). Applies `width=` as an
    inline `style` on the `<img>`; warns and drops `caption=`/`id=` (no
    `<figure>` inline to caption or number) and any unknown attribute name.
    Never leaves the braces in the output.
  - Braces with no `=` in them (`the set {a, b}`) are prose, not a suffix, and
    pass through completely untouched.
- `docs/markdown.md` "Width and captions": documents the inline behaviour.
- `changelog.d/inline-image-attrs.fixed.md`.

## Rendering paths checked (item 4)

`_apply_figure_attrs` is called from exactly two places, and every rendered
body in the site comes from one of them:

- `render_bodies` — build.py:1855, item bodies (`item.body_html`).
- `render_pages` — build.py:1886, narrative page bodies (`page.body_html`).

Everything downstream consumes those two fields, so log entries
(`templates/log.html.j2:41-42`, fed by `render.py:910`, `:1006`, `:1077`),
item pages (`item.html.j2:125-126`, `render.py:941`), document pages
(`document.html.j2:77-78`, `render.py:953`), per-board and per-workspace
documents (`render.py:983`, `:1055`) and narrative pages (`page.html.j2:19`,
`render.py:863`) all get the fix for free. The thread panel
(`render.py:260-348`, `item.html.j2:36-70`) renders verdict fields and entry
references only — no `body_html` at all — so there is no image path there to
fix.
