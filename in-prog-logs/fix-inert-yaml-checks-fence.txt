## The diagnostic

Placed in `src/refdes/build.py`, `validate_items` — the same place body
warnings are raised (the `body: is empty` warning is two lines above;
`calc.fence_errors` reports at `item.body_line + offset` the same way). New
module-level `_YAML_FENCE_RE` (```yaml/```yml fences, fence run 3+, language
attributes tolerated) and `declares_inert_keys(body, field_names)`:
(0-indexed fence line, declared-field keys) for every yaml fence in the body
whose content parses as a mapping naming a field the item's own type declares.
`validate_items` turns each into a `project.warn` at the fence line naming the
key, the type, and the fix ("move it into the item's front matter").

Narrow by construction: non-mapping fences, unparseable fences, fences with no
declared-field key, and keys that are fields of *other* types never fire. The
body text is never modified (rendering unchanged).
