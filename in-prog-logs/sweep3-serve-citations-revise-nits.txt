E1 APPLIES. serve/edit.py:722 `except LockUnavailable` in `_accept_pins`, while
   the section at ~465-473 catches `OSError` and `_disk_write_lock`'s docstring
   (edit.py:288-289) says the OSError "every caller here" raises is turned into
   a refusal. `project_write_lock` (write_lock.py) does raise raw OSErrors
   outside LockUnavailable: `os.stat(root)` and the walk's `os.stat(directory)`
   (write_lock.py:42-46) are unwrapped; only open/flock failures are wrapped.
   Decided by reading the callers: `apply_edit`'s contract is "authoring
   outcomes are values; only a genuinely unexpected internal fault raises"
   (edit.py docstring) and api.py `_apply_edit`/`_create_item` call the entry
   points with no OSError handler (a raised OSError becomes a 500). So the
   DOCS are right and the CATCH is wrong: broaden _accept_pins to OSError.
   The same read of callers also indicts `create_item` (edit.py:1054): its
   `with _disk_write_lock(root)` has no handler at all, and a probe
   (.scratch/e1-probe/test_probe.py, run under pytest) confirmed
   `create_item` on a read-only root with writable child raises
   `refdes.write_lock.LockUnavailable` out of the entry point instead of
   returning `Refused`, violating both its own "Returns Created | Refused |
   Invalid" docstring and the _disk_write_lock "every caller" claim. Fixed
   both; tests in tests/test_citations_lockfile_locking.py (existing
   read-only-root accept test is the sibling).

E2 APPLIES (add comment). `_LockfileWrite.rollback` (edit.py:517-520) clears
   `self.written` before taking the lock. Cannot lose a rollback: `written` is
   per-instance memory; the instance never escapes its `_Acceptance`; the only
   caller of rollback is `_undo_accept` (edit.py:602), reached at most once per
   acceptance on the same thread under `write_lock_for`. No second reader of
   the flag exists, in-thread or cross-process. Added a comment saying so.
